# Lessons Learned (May–Oct 2026)

What kept costing money or time, and what to do about it.

## 1. Hidden roof layers (4 jobs!)
Studer (4 layers), Ferguson (3 incl. cedar shake), Najera, Wescher (2) all turned up more layers than sold.
→ **Ask the homeowner about layers before signing. Put a per-layer upcharge clause in the contract.** "Complete tear-off" with no layer caveat bit us on Studer.

## 2. Bad measurements from sales
Brianna Green (Zillow dims), Candreva (3,000 vs 3,800), James Wilson (satellite only), Sheffield (flat deck counted as roof), Dowling (wrong wall from the homeowner).
→ **Re-measure with EagleView/Roofr or a site visit before ordering.** Never trust Zillow.

## 3. Underbidding
Brady and other sales underbid a lot (Wayne Posey, Grace Moe, Clifton stone, Dowling to beat a competitor). Steven usually keeps underwater jobs going instead of refunding.
→ Run the margin check (40–50%) the day a job lands. Flag early.

## 4. Dumpsters are always the bottleneck
Studer, Candreva, Debbie Jensen, Pryor, Clifton.
→ **Book the dumpster the same day the contractor is booked.** Confirm the delivery the day before.

## 5. Pay subs when you say you will
Ron Raymond (Brianna Green) got angry over late pay. Richard (Pryor) was upset about timing. Card declines on Trey Pierce.
→ Get Eddy set up *before* the job starts. Zelle caps at $600.

## 6. Payment collection drags
Bernhardt's PennyMac partial checks took ~2 months. Habitat checks were slow.
→ For lender/escrow-paid jobs, get the lender contact on day 1.

## 7. Mass outreach works, sometimes
Blasting 15–25 local contractors gets a couple of replies at best (zero for the Lima electrician). Indeed applicants have been better lately.
→ Use the Indeed ad format. **Never include the street address** in blasts.

## 8. Scope gaps are my fault too
Pryor soffit/fascia never made it into Richard's scope. Maranatha bid only demo because I under-described Phase 1.
→ Send every sub a written scope summary checked line by line against the sold contract.

## 9. Calls
On the Legacy/Colazo call I led with bad news and loss framing, and they took the refund.
→ **Lead with options**, use the leverage (exclusivity, price lock), and ask about competing bids early.

## 10. Data hygiene
Notion statuses go stale (Clifton, Pryor, Candreva). The daily loop is only as good as the cards.
