# Texting Voice & Hard Rules

## Phone Lines (never cross them)
- **Homeowners →** my line, **435-681-3428**
- **Contractors →** Alexis's line, **928-691-4549**
- October problem: contractor texts (Tim, Richard Scott, countertop applicants, William) went out from or landed on my homeowner line

## Hard Rules
- Always write **"Sentri Homes."** Never "Century"
- **40% margin floor.** Below that = caution/stop. Extra scope = change order
- Never tell a homeowner what a sub is paid, or a sub what the homeowner pays
- Never guarantee payment before approval. No threats, blaming by name, venting, or personal excuses
- **Refunds go to Steven + Donald.** Don't discuss them with homeowners
- Every promise gets a date
- First contractor text: trade, scope, and town only. Send the address after they're locked in
- Before a sub starts: signed agreement + insurance + W-9 on file
- Bots draft, I approve and send. Shortcut approvals: "send Greg", "send Pryor", "order Pryor"

## Texting Voice (written 10/16)
- 1–3 sentences, one topic, friendly and direct
- "Hey [First name]," and on first contact: "Hey [Name], this is Bryan from Sentri Homes."
- Lead with the news or the ask. Use real dates, $ amounts, sq ft
- No emojis unless they use them first. Never curse
- Don't know yet? Give a time when I will

## How I Want Help
- Short, blunt rollups: money first, then what's blocked, then what I need to decide
- A batch of drafts I can edit
- Tone: head-of-desk, money-focused, warm not corporate
- Keep Sentri and school completely separate
