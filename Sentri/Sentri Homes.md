## Rules & Policies
- **Margin target: 40–50% gross**
- Deposits to subs need **Monte or Steven** sign-off
- Subs need a Secretary of State check + COI. Contractor agreement signed by Alonso
- "Sentri method" = hire individual contractors off Indeed (more margin than hiring out a contracting company)
- **Never put the full street address in mass outreach texts**, city/area only
- Nothing touching money goes out without a human reading it

## Indeed Ad Format (standard)
1. Job title
2. "Job Location: City, State"
3. Fixed company intro (nationwide residential construction co, project-by-project contractors, great crews become the go-to team)
4. **SCOPE OF WORK** bullets
5. **JOB DETAILS** bullets: start date, duration, hours, site details, point of contact
6. Fixed **REQUIREMENTS**: 2+ yrs trade experience, own tools, reliable transportation + debris disposal, before/during/after photos
7. **PAY**: rate or range, Net 7
8. Fixed closing line about repeat projects nationwide
9. Contact info (PM name, number, email) + "Please contact me directly if you have any questions"
