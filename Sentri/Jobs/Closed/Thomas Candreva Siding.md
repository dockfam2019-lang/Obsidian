# Thomas Candreva: Hardie Siding (Prattsville, NY)

**Outcome: cancelled + refunded. The roughest job of the summer.**

- 3714 County Route 10, Prattsville NY 12468. Client Thomas Candreva Jr. (78, VA vet). Son **Mitchell** (union carpenter, 20 yrs) handles decisions
- **Labor-only** whole-house James Hardie install. Client bought materials (G&H Lumber Greenville, ask for Dylan)
- ~$26,911 after 5% military discount. EagleView later showed **~3,800 sq ft vs 3,000 quoted**
- Sales agent Avery introduced Sentri as "Century Homes", which confused the client

## What Happened
1. **8/12**: Jamelle Gibson (Albany) hired at ~$16K
2. **8/24–25**: no-show twice, then said he couldn't do it for the price. My 8/25 call with Thomas saved the job
3. **8/26**: Jamelle's *second* crew showed up and started
4. **8/28**: the back-door mini-roof was dropped (not buildable as scoped), $1,000 credit
5. **9/3**: trim mitering looked bad (plastic trim, skill saw, no ladder)
6. **9/9**: **Mitchell fired the crew**: wrong nails, miters, 2nd floor before 1st, siding 1⅜" out of level
7. **9/18**: decided to cancel and refund. Steven took it over
8. **10/4**: Cancelled in Notion

## Lessons
- Vet labor-only crews hard. Make them actually see the site before quoting
- A sharp, knowledgeable family member will audit everything, so tight QC from day 1
- Re-measure before signing (3,000 → 3,800)
