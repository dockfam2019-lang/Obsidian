#### Exchange 8

**User request(s)**

````text
I went through the Today, School, Money, Nutrition, Training, Running, Reading, Sleep, Goals and Progress pages. The design is clean and consistent, with a calm dark theme, a clear one-section-per-area layout, and friendly empty states. What it lacks is polish and connection between the pages. I haven’t tested it on a phone, so I can’t say how it holds up there.

**Bugs and things that look broken**

- The School page header says “Friday, October 18”. Every other page says October 16, which is the correct date. Use one shared date function for all headers.
- The Goals page has “Test school”, “Test workout” and “Test check-in” buttons in the Your data card, with a red “Clear all tracker data” button right beside them. Hide the test buttons behind a dev flag. Move the clear button into a settings page and make it require typing “DELETE”, or at least export a backup first.
- Your daily habits don’t match. Today shows “Move your body / Read a little / Check your spending / Pick tomorrow’s priority”, while Goals shows “Check in with your money / Set tomorrow up”. They should come from one list that you can edit.
- The streak says 3 days while today’s habits are 0 of 4. Make it clear whether the streak counts today yet.
- On the Sleep chart, the y-axis labels (3.4, 7.5, 5.5) don’t line up with the 9.0-hour night. Check the axis scaling. Also, a 9-hour night rated “Rough” with 1/5 restfulness is worth a hint like “long sleep, low rest, check the time you went to bed”.

**Data entry**

- Class times are free text, which is how you ended up with “12am” (it should be pm), “1;00 pm” and “4;00 Pm”. Replace them with day and time pickers, and then use that data. The School and Today pages could show “next class in 2h” and warn you about homework due before the next class.
- Homework has no priority, due time or estimated duration, and no way to attach it to a grade.
- Grades only appear after you tap “+ Grade” on finished work. Show each class’s current grade on its card, so the progress bar means something.

**Making it more useful as a tracker**

- Right now the pages sit side by side without talking to each other. The Today page could say things like “Legs day tomorrow, you slept 9h but rated it rough” or “Protein is at 0g with 3 hours left, so log food”. Add a daily summary or score at the top that pulls from all areas.
- Logging takes too many taps. Add a quick-add bar on Today, a floating “+” button that works from any page, keyboard shortcuts, and “repeat yesterday’s meal”.
- Training: show sets, reps and weight so you can log what you lifted. Once those are logged, add progression tracking (for example “last time 3×10 at 50 lb”) and a rest timer. The Log buttons are greyed out until the day arrives, so let people log late or ahead.
- Money is empty, so it feels dead. Start with a few suggested default budgets, offer a CSV import, and make rent or subscriptions a one-tap setup. Otherwise this page is just zeros.
- Progress charts have almost no data yet, so they show single dots and short lines. Hide a chart until it has at least 5 points, or show “need 5 more days” so it doesn’t look broken.
- Goals: “chess 1000 elo” has no target date, no current value and no progress. Add a metric, a current value, a target and a deadline, plus a progress bar. Make it count something, for example weekly puzzles solved or games played.
- Reminders: use web push or an email nudge for homework due tomorrow and for a missed daily check-in.

**Design and usability polish**

- Text is small and low-contrast in many places (grey on dark). Raise the body text size and contrast to meet accessibility standards.
- Completed items should collapse after a day or two, and overdue items should be red and sorted to the top.
- Add search, filters and sorting on the School and Nutrition lists.
- The sidebar has 10 items. Group them (Daily, Body, Life) or move Reading, Sleep and Running under one “Health” area.
- Add undo toasts after delete. The × buttons currently look like they delete right away.
- Give the empty states one clear action rather than two competing buttons (there is “Add” at the top and again in the card).
- Your backups are in Goals, buried at the bottom. The footer says it syncs, but it isn’t clear whether it’s saved or the last sync time. Show “Saved ✓ 2:41 pm”.
- Page titles are catchy, but the app name and tagline in the corner could carry more branding.

**What I’d do first**

1. Fix the date bug and remove or hide the test buttons and the “Clear all data” button.
2. Merge the two habit lists into one.
3. Replace free-text class times with pickers.
4. Add a quick-add button and a daily score on Today that ties the pages together.

I can also build any of these for you if you have the source code. Send me the repo or the files and I’ll start with the first four fixes.
````
