````text
If a car could travel 1000 mph with infinite gas could it stay in daylight all year long
````

**Assistant**

````text
**Yes—in theory, if it could drive west wherever it needed to, including across oceans.** Going west lets it chase the Sun and delay sunset.

The speed needed depends on latitude:

- **At the equator:** about **1,037 mph**, so 1,000 mph is slightly too slow.
- **At 30° latitude:** about **900 mph**, so it’s fast enough to keep the Sun in roughly the same position in the sky.

To stay in daylight **all year**, it could also move gradually north and south with the seasons, avoiding winter darkness.

So a hypothetical car with unrestricted travel could do it. **A normal car following actual roads couldn’t.**
````
