````text
On my MacBook Pro m5 I have two monitors I’m trying to connect a 34 inch curved and a 27 inch curved monitor so do I have to have one hdmi go to the native port and one go to my dongle port
````

**Assistant**

````text
**Yes—one HDMI into your MacBook’s built-in port and the other into your USB-C dongle is a valid setup**, assuming the dongle supports HDMI video output.

- **34-inch:** HDMI directly into the MacBook.
- **27-inch:** HDMI into the dongle, then dongle into a USB-C port.

Your M5 supports **two external monitors while keeping the laptop screen working**, too. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

You don’t *have* to use that arrangement, but it’s straightforward with your equipment. In **System Settings → Displays**, choose **Extended display** so each screen gives you separate workspace.
````

#### Exchange 2

**User**

````text
So I can’t do it with two dongles ?
````

**Assistant**

````text
**Yes, you can use two dongles**—plug each USB-C-to-HDMI dongle into a separate USB-C port on your MacBook, then connect one monitor to each.

You don’t need to use the built-in HDMI port.
````
