**Assistant**

````text
**Yes—you can frame the house above the basement first, then pump the basement floor concrete through the bulkhead entrance later.** Pouring basement slabs after the house is enclosed is a normal construction sequence. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

A few things need to be planned:

- **Basement partition walls:** Generally pour the slab before framing these.
- **Structural supports:** Posts and bearing walls supporting the house need their designed footings/supports before framing above.
- **Under the slab:** Finish plumbing, compacted stone, vapor barrier, any insulation, and required inspections before pouring.
- **Backfilling:** Follow the foundation’s bracing requirements—some designs depend on the slab for support at the bottom of the walls. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

So **you don’t necessarily have to wait on the basement floor to start framing the house.**
````
