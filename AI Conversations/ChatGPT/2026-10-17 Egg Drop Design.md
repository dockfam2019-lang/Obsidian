````text
How hard would it be to build a contraption for an egg to survive a drop from 20 ft
````

**Assistant**

````text
**Pretty manageable—20 feet is a very doable egg-drop challenge**, especially if you’re allowed a parachute and plenty of padding.

The trick is to **slow the egg down gradually** instead of letting it stop suddenly against the ground.

A simple design would use:

- **A small cardboard box** with the egg centered inside.
- **Soft foam or loosely crumpled paper** around every side, keeping the egg away from the walls.
- **A crush zone underneath**, like accordion-folded cardboard or paper cups, to absorb the landing.
- **A large plastic-bag parachute**, if allowed, attached evenly to the box’s corners.

Without a parachute, it’s still very achievable—you’ll just need better cushioning and a structure that can crumple on impact. You could build a basic version in **30–60 minutes**, then improve it through test drops.

**What materials and size limits does your assignment allow, and are parachutes permitted?**
````
