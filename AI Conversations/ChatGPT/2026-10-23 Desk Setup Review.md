What do you think of the setup a big 6 foot standing desk adjustable a Logitech mx keys s and Logitech mx keys 4 for the mouse and a leather desk mat a 27 inch curved monitor to the right in the middle a 34 inch curved monitor and then on the left side either my MacBook Pro or MacBook Air

**Assistant**

````text
**That sounds like a clean, comfortable setup**, and a 6-foot desk should give you enough room for all three screens. The MX Keys S, MX Master mouse, and leather mat would pull it together nicely.

A few things I’d prioritize:

- **Center the 34-inch directly in front of you**, with the keyboard centered under it. That’s your main workspace.
- **Angle the 27-inch inward on the right.** Great for messages, reference material, or a second application.
- **Put the MacBook on a raised stand on the left**, so you aren’t constantly looking down at it. I’d use whichever Mac you work on most.
- **Check desk depth.** Around **30 inches deep** would make this arrangement much more comfortable; width alone won’t prevent the screens from feeling too close.
- **Use monitor arms if the screens support them.** They free up space and make adjusting the arrangement easier.

The main tradeoff is how wide everything gets—you’ll want your everyday work on the middle monitor so you aren’t constantly turning your head. **Otherwise, that’s a setup I’d be happy to work at all day.**
````

**Assistant**

````text
That’ll complete the setup nicely—**having both monitors on arms will free up desk space and make aligning them easier.**

For the 27-inch, check:

- **VESA compatibility:** usually 75×75 or 100×100 mm.
- **Weight capacity:** use the monitor’s weight without its stand.
- **Enough adjustment:** you’ll want to angle it inward and bring it close to the 34-inch’s edge.

**A separate single arm is a good choice** since you already have one for the 34-inch. It gives you flexibility with positioning and clamp placement.
````
