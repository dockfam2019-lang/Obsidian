**The biggest changes: shave with your neck’s hair growth and avoid shaving too close.** Neck hairs can grow sideways or upward, so shaving downward everywhere may actually go against the grain. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

Try this routine:

1. **Soften the hair** with a warm shower, then apply fragrance-free shaving cream for sensitive skin.
2. **Check the growth direction** before shaving and follow it in each section.
3. **Use a clean, sharp single- or double-blade razor**, light pressure, and short strokes. Don’t stretch your skin or repeatedly go over the same spot.
4. **Finish with a cool, damp washcloth and fragrance-free moisturizer.** Avoid alcohol-based aftershave. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

**If bumps keep coming back, use a guarded electric trimmer leaving about 1 mm of stubble.** Give the irritated area a break from close shaving, and don’t pick or tweeze the bumps. Persistent, painful, or pus-filled bumps should be checked by a clinician. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

Are you currently using a cartridge razor or an electric shaver—and do you shave upward on your neck?
