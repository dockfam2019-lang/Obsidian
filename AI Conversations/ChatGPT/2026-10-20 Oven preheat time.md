That looks like a **double conventional oven with convection features**, not a microwave. “Conv. Roast” means **convection roast**.

To preheat:

1. Press **Bake** under **UPPER** or **LOWER**, depending on which oven you’re using.
2. Enter the temperature with the number buttons—for example, **3 → 5 → 0** for 350°F.
3. Press **Start**. That button appears to be outside the photo, likely farther right.
4. Wait for the preheat beep before putting your food in.

Use regular **Bake** for standard oven instructions.
