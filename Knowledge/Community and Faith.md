# Community and Faith

## The Community
Bryan lives in Centennial Park, AZ — a tight-knit religious community in the desert. Centennial Park Academy is the private school attached to it.

## Mission
After high school, the plan is to serve a mission — standard path for young men in the community. This comes before the business grind, not instead of it.

## Faith
Jesus is the only person Bryan actively looks up to. Not performative — just honest.

## Influence on Mindset
Mr. Walter Hammon taught church history but really taught structure and how to live. That stuck.
